The Problem

Why Existing Government Portals & Helplines Fail

Citizens who rely on welfare schemes face language, technical, and interface barriers every single day.

🌐

English-Centric Portals & Jargon

Citizens seeking simple Aadhaar updates or ration cards must navigate multi-layered web forms in English with complex CAPTCHAs, forcing heavy reliance on middlemen and cyber cafes.

📵

Robotic IVRs & Harsh 5s Disconnects

Conventional phone IVR systems disconnect if the caller pauses for 5 seconds. Elderly callers who hesitate to articulate their questions get cut off abruptly, causing intense anxiety and distress.

🗣️

Regional Accent Breakdown

Standard Western voice bots fail completely on colloquial Indian regional languages and mixed code-switched queries like "Aadhaar address maatha enna documents venum?".

How I Solved It

Engineering Valli: Human-Centric AI Innovations

A purpose-built conversational voice architecture tailored for Indian regional helpline callers.

🌍

1-Click Regional Language Switching

5 Languages

Users can select Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, or Kannada from the UI. The session automatically synchronizes STT language code, TTS synthesis voice, system prompt instructions, and regional greetings in real-time.

👂

45-Second Patient Listening Window

Empathetic UX

Unlike harsh IVRs that hang up after 5 seconds, Valli waits a full 45 seconds after her speech finishes before gently checking in with a localized "Hello? Ungalukku ketkutha?" or "Hello? Kya aap mujhe sun sakte hain?".

⏱️

Language-Adaptive Acoustic Fillers

Zero Dead Air

To eliminate dead air while the LLM generates tokens, Valli plays an immediate filler clip customized to the active language: "ஒரு நிமிஷம்..." (Tamil), "एक मिनट रुकिए..." (Hindi), "ఒక్క నిమిషం ఉండండి..." (Telugu), "ഒരു നിമിഷം നിൽക്കൂ..." (Malayalam), or "ಒಂದು ನಿಮಿಷ ಇರಿ..." (Kannada).

🌐

Distraction-Free Direct Visit Card

Clean UI

All overwhelming English text dumps and complicated checklist items are stripped from the phone UI. The card surfaces only the direct official portal button for seamless 1-tap navigation.

Technology Architecture

Tech Stack & The Strategic Pivot to Groq

Engineered for extreme responsiveness, fault tolerance, and zero infrastructure cost bottlenecks.

⚡

Why Groq + openai/gpt-oss-20b?

Key Architectural Decision

Deploying enterprise 30B MoE models (such as gnani/gnani-evon-v3.3-30B-A3B) requires over 63 GB of unquantized VRAM or expensive dedicated GPU clusters, which leads to out-of-memory crashes on free/commodity tiers.

By deploying on Groq's LPU Inference Engine with `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, we achieved an astonishing 65+ tokens/second with sub-400ms Time-To-First-Token (TTFT). This guarantees natural, instantaneous phone conversation turns with zero latency lag across all 5 regional languages.

STT Transport

Gnani Prisma STT

Continuous WebSocket streaming with native Indic acoustic models (ta-IN, hi-IN, te-IN, ml-IN, kn-IN).

Reasoning Core

Groq gpt-oss-20b

Ultra-low latency LPU inference with strict grounded system prompt instructions and conversational memory preservation across follow-up turns.

Voice Synthesis

Gnani Timbre v3.0

Warm Lakshmi voice in 16 kHz mono WAV. Supports all 5 Indic languages with dynamic websockets header resolution and <700 char chunking.

Web Full-Duplex

FastAPI + WebSockets

Dual-channel server routing binary mic PCM to STT while returning binary TTS WAV segments and JSON control frames to the browser.

System Flow

End-to-End Multilingual Turn-Taking Pipeline

From microphone input to regional spoken audio output in under 1 second.

🎙️
1. AudioWorklet

Captures mic audio & downsamples to 16 kHz PCM mono in real-time.

🌊
2. Prisma STT

Continuous stream emits partials & final transcript in the selected regional language.

⚡
3. Groq + Filler Buffer

Fires instant filler audio while Groq LPU streams localized response tokens.

✂️
4. TagBuffer Chunker

Segments clauses (<700 chars) for incremental Timbre TTS synthesis.

🔊
5. Synced Output & Portal

Plays synthesized speech smoothly while official website portal button appears.
